# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Project Management Processes Summary
OctoAcme follows a structured project lifecycle designed to move work from idea to measurable outcomes: Initiation, Planning, Execution & Tracking, Release & Deployment, and Retrospective & Continuous Improvement. Teams begin with a project one-pager that defines the problem, objective, success metrics, stakeholders, and key risks, then use a go/no-go gate to confirm readiness before detailed planning starts.

During planning, OctoAcme turns approved work into shippable increments by creating a prioritized backlog with clear acceptance criteria, estimates, dependency tracking, and a documented Definition of Done. Project teams align on milestones and release plans early, while risk and dependency management is handled through a maintained risk register reviewed throughout delivery.

Role clarity is central to execution. Project Managers coordinate schedules, dependencies, risks, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize value; Developers implement and test solutions; and QA validates acceptance criteria and release readiness. Daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, sprint demos, and regular stakeholder updates keep teams aligned, while escalation paths ensure blockers and incidents are handled quickly.

Quality assurance is embedded across the workflow through CI checks, linting, unit and integration testing, smoke testing for critical flows, manual QA when needed, and required review/approval before merge. Before each release, teams confirm acceptance criteria, passing security scans, rollback readiness, and post-deploy verification. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, and incidents convert lessons learned into tracked action items that strengthen future delivery.
